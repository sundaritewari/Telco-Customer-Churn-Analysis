# 📊 Telco Customer Churn Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing customer churn data from a telecom company to uncover the core reasons why customers leave and identify high-risk consumer segments. By combining SQL for robust data analytics and Power BI for dynamic visual storytelling, this interactive dashboard provides actionable insights to drive customer retention strategies and safeguard revenue.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
- SQL / MySQL Server: Used for initial data exploration, advanced database querying, data cleaning, and business insight discovery.
- Power BI Desktop: Used for building a premium dark-themed interactive dashboard layout.
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions): Utilized to calculate complex custom metrics such as Total Customers, Churn Rate, and Monthly Charges at Risk.
- Advanced Power BI Features: Implemented Bookmarks and custom Selection Actions to build minimalist, interactive clickable text-button filters for seamless navigation.

## 📝 Key Business Insights
- Contract Risk: Customers on a Month-to-month contract exhibit the highest propensity to churn compared to those on one or two-year plans.
- Infrastructure Drivers: Users subscribed to Fiber Optic internet service are leaving at a significantly higher rate than DSL users, indicating potential issues in pricing premium tiers or service quality.
- Revenue at Risk: A substantial portion of monthly charges is currently categorized under high-risk churn segments, demanding immediate marketing intervention.

## 💡 Strategic Recommendations
1. Contract Conversion: Design attractive promotional incentives or loyalty discounts to transition high-risk month-to-month subscribers into stable, long-term annual contracts.
2. Technical Audit: Conduct an immediate operational and infrastructure review of the Fiber Optic tier to detect and resolve network stability issues or price-to-value dissatisfaction.
3. Proactive Retention: Set up an automated early-warning system targeting customers within their first 12 months (0-12 tenure group) with tailored retention offers.
